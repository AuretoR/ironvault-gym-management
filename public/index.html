GOOD TO KNOW

COMMON QUESTIONS.

I'm new to the gym. Can I join?

Yes. Choose a membership and tell us your preferred program. Ask the gym about orientation and coaching options.

Can I change my membership plan?

Plan changes depend on the gym's policy. The admin can manage plans and membership records in the management system.

How do I renew?

Contact the gym team. Authorized staff can renew a member's plan and record the payment in the admin system.

Are reminders sent automatically?

Reminders require a configured email, WhatsApp or SMS provider. The system must never show a reminder as sent until delivery is confirmed.
